Introduction to the Mily Unlimited ecosystem

Mily Unlimited is a one-person project — driven by a passion for electronics, programming, and building things from scratch. The ecosystem includes several related platforms and tools that together form an environment for people building electronics projects.

What does the ecosystem include?

  • MB&SP (Miły's Buy & Sell Parts) — the flagship platform with a catalog of electronic components, a shopping cart, quotes, and order handling. Available at mbsp.com.pl
  • CzatAI — an experimental conversational assistant integrated with MB&SP. It helps with catalog navigation and answers technical questions. Currently at Beta 1.0.
  • MB Designer — a lightweight, browser-based electronic schematic editor. A tool for people who design circuits and want to quickly document a schematic without installing any software.
  • Maker Tools — a set of calculators and tools useful for electronics projects.

Ecosystem sites

  • milyunlimited.eu — Mily Unlimited homepage
  • business.milyunlimited.eu — Mily Unlimited: For Business - a site presenting my technical expertise, services, and opportunities to work with me

How to use the Help Center

The Mily Unlimited Help Center is divided into sections corresponding to individual platforms. Choose the topic you’re interested in from the menu on the left. If you can’t find an answer to your question, get in touch through the contact page.

Versions and stage of development

All platforms in the ecosystem are under active development. Some features are in Beta or planned for upcoming updates. You can find the detailed development status in the project changelog. The project is run transparently — changes, bugs, and plans are documented publicly.

What is CzatAI

CzatAI is a built-in conversational assistant available on the MB&SP.com.pl platform. Its role is shopping support — it helps you navigate the catalog, answers questions about products and availability, and also serves an informational and educational function for makers and electronics engineers.

The current version, CzatAI 1.0 Beta, runs on local keyword-processing algorithms and a closed intent database — it is not a generative language model (LLM). This means the bot understands specific queries and phrases from its database, but doesn’t hold free-form conversations the way ChatGPT does, for example.

CzatAI is an actively developed project. In the future, we plan to introduce advanced language models that will significantly expand the assistant’s capabilities.

What is CzatAI for?

  • Searching for products and components in the MB&SP catalog
  • Answers to questions about orders, shipping, and returns
  • Technical and educational tips on electronics and maker projects
  • Help choosing components (resistors, capacitors, integrated circuits)
  • Generating example code snippets for microcontroller platforms

Important: All technical answers are for illustrative and educational purposes only. Always verify information against the manufacturer’s official documentation (datasheet) before using it in a project.

Limits and availability

CzatAI is available directly on the MB&SP.com.pl site, with no need to log in or create an account. There is no hard limit on the number of messages per session — feel free to ask as many questions as you like.

Knowledge base limitations

Because the current Beta version runs on a closed intent database (it is not a generative model), the bot has a limited scope of knowledge and only understands queries similar to those it was prepared for in advance. If a question falls outside its database, CzatAI will let you know it has no answer and suggest contacting support.

What CzatAI doesn’t support (Beta version)

  • Free-form conversation and questions unrelated to electronics or the MB&SP platform
  • Queries in languages other than Polish
  • Access to your account data or order history
  • Placing orders or processing payments
  • Complex multi-step technical analyses

Data and privacy in a session

In Beta 1.0, queries are processed only locally in the browser’s session memory and are not permanently stored on MB&SP.com.pl servers. When you close the window, the chat history is deleted. Do not enter personal data, passwords, or confidential information into the chat window.

Introduction to MB Designer

MB Designer is a simple, browser-based tool for drawing schematic diagrams of electronic circuits. It works without installation — just open the page and start drawing.

The tool is designed for quickly sketching concepts and simple illustrative schematics — it doesn’t replace professional EDA software (such as KiCad or Eagle) and doesn’t support advanced features such as simulation, electrical analysis, or PCB design.

What you can do in MB Designer

  • Draw simple schematic diagrams of electronic circuits
  • Export a schematic to PNG or PDF
  • Save and load projects in JSON format (a local file on your device)

What MB Designer doesn’t support

  • Advanced EDA features (simulation, DRC, PCB design)
  • Importing external schematic formats (e.g. KiCad, Eagle)
  • Saving projects on a server — everything stays local in your browser

MB Designer Terms

§1. Nature of the Service

MB Designer is a free tool for drawing electronic schematics, provided by Mily Unlimited. Using the tool does not require creating an account or having an MB&SP account.

§2. Data and Project Storage

MB Designer does not save any schematics or projects on Mily Unlimited servers. All project data exists only locally — in the browser’s session memory or in files saved by the user on their device. Closing the browser tab without saving the project to a JSON file results in permanent data loss.

§3. File Format

The only supported format for saving and loading projects is JSON. MB Designer does not support importing or exporting to external schematic formats (e.g. KiCad, Eagle, Altium). Schematics can only be exported as a PNG image or a PDF document — for illustrative purposes only.

§4. Scope of Features

MB Designer is a tool for creating simple schematic diagrams. It has no electrical analysis, circuit simulation, or PCB design features, nor does it verify schematic correctness. The tool’s output is for illustrative purposes only.

§5. Liability

Mily Unlimited is not liable for errors in schematics created using MB Designer or for any damage resulting from using those schematics in electronics projects. The user is obliged to independently verify the correctness of schematics before using them.

§6. Changes to the Tool

The Service Provider reserves the right to modify, restrict, or discontinue MB Designer at any time without prior notice.

Privacy

Protecting your privacy matters to us. The Mily Unlimited ecosystem is designed to collect as little data as possible — we have no account system on the main site, we don’t sell data, and we don’t show ads.

Key principles at a glance

  • Mily Unlimited homepage — collects only anonymous GA4 analytics data (only with your consent). Display preferences (light/dark mode) are stored locally in your browser.
  • MB&SP — the shopping platform processes the data necessary to fulfill orders (name, address, email). See the MB&SP terms for details.
  • CzatAI — queries are processed locally in the browser session and are not saved on a server.
  • MB Designer — schematics are never sent to a server. Everything stays local on your device.

You can find the full information in the Privacy Policy and the Cookie Policy.

What data we process

The Mily Unlimited homepage has no contact forms and no account registration system. Data is processed only in the following cases:

Data collected by Google Analytics may be transferred to Google LLC in the United States. Google states that it applies appropriate GDPR compliance mechanisms for such transfers (e.g. standard contractual clauses). You can find details on how Google processes data in Google’s privacy policy.

You can find the full list of cookies used and their purposes in the Cookie Policy section.

Legal basis for processing

Analytics data is processed on the basis of your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by interacting with the cookie banner shown on your first visit to the site. You can change your decision at any time by clearing your browser data for this site — the banner will be shown again on your next visit.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to: access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict its processing, data portability, and object to processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO). To exercise these rights, contact us at the address given in the “Data controller” section.

Because the site has no user account system, most personal data (such as the IP address within Google Analytics) is processed in an anonymized or pseudonymized form by Google Analytics itself and is not directly linked by us to any specific, identified person.

Data retention period

Analytics data in Google Analytics 4 is stored for a period consistent with the service configuration (by default up to 14 months), after which it is automatically deleted or anonymized by Google. Your light/dark mode choice saved in your browser remains there until you change it or clear your browsing data.

Cookie Policy

This policy describes how the sites of the Mily Unlimited ecosystem (milyunlimited.eu, mbsp.com.pl) use cookies and similar browser data storage technologies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a site. They allow sites to remember your preferences and behavior between sessions — e.g. your chosen color theme or the fact that you accepted the cookie banner.

Which cookies do we use?

Essential (technical)

Stored only in the browser’s localStorage. They do not require consent.

  • mu-theme — remembers your chosen theme (light / dark)
  • mu-cookie-consent — remembers that you accepted the cookie banner so it doesn’t show on every visit
  • mbsp-cart — contents of the shopping cart on MB&SP (local data, not sent to the server)

Analytics (optional)

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) for anonymous traffic analysis — number of visits, popularity of subpages, and approximate geographic location. GA4 is loaded only after you give consent via the cookie banner. Data is processed by Google LLC in accordance with its privacy policy.

  • GA4 session identifiers are anonymized — they do not identify you personally
  • Analytics data is not sold or shared with third parties
  • You can opt out of analytics at any time (see below)

How to manage cookies

You can withdraw your consent or manage cookies at any time in several ways:

  • Clear site data in your browser settings — the cookie banner will appear again on your next visit, giving you the chance to choose again
  • Google Analytics Opt-out add-on — tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout — blocks GA4 tracking across all sites
  • Incognito / private mode — localStorage data is not saved permanently

Policy changes

The cookie policy may be updated as the platform evolves. We will notify you of significant changes through a banner on the site. Last updated: July 2026.

GDPR — Your rights

Data controller

The controller of your personal data is Miłosz Boguniewicz, who runs the Mily Unlimited project. Contact: hello@milyunlimited.eu. You can find detailed information about what data we process and for what purpose in the Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)

You have the right to obtain confirmation from us as to whether we process your personal data and, if so, access to that data and information about the purposes of processing, categories of data, recipients, and the planned storage period.

Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)

You have the right to request the prompt rectification of inaccurate personal data concerning you, or the completion of incomplete data.

Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)

You have the right to request the erasure of your personal data (the so-called “right to be forgotten”) if the data is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, you have withdrawn your consent, or you have successfully objected to the processing.

Please note that analytics data in Google Analytics is processed by Google — erasing it requires contacting Google directly or using the Google Analytics blocking add-on. Data stored locally in your browser (theme choice, cookie consent) can be deleted by you by clearing site data in your browser settings.

Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)

You have the right to request restriction of the processing of your data in the cases specified in the GDPR, e.g. when you contest the accuracy of the data or have objected to the processing.

Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)

You have the right to receive the personal data you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

You have the right to object at any time to the processing of your personal data based on the controller’s legitimate interest. Following an objection, we will not process your data unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing.

Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

If processing is based on your consent (e.g. consent to analytics cookies), you have the right to withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. To withdraw your consent to cookies, clear the site data in your browser settings — the cookie banner will appear again on your next visit.
