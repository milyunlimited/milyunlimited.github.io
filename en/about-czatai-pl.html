About the project

Simple support while using MB&SP

CzatAI was created as an experimental tool to support users of the MB&SP platform.

Its goal is to make the service easier to use by helping you find information faster and reach help more easily.

What can it do?

Answers questions

Helps you find information available on the platform.

Helps you use MB&SP

Explains basic features and guides users through selected parts of the service.

Supports the Help Center

Can point you to relevant articles or documentation.

Why was it built?

MB&SP grows with every new version.

CzatAI is one of the experiments testing whether artificial intelligence can make the MB&SP platform simpler and more practical to use.

Current development

Available

  • Basic conversations
  • Platform help
  • Help Center integration

In progress

  • Better answers
  • Deeper knowledge of the platform
  • Integration with more MB&SP features

Notice

CzatAI is an experimental project. Answers may be incomplete or contain errors, so for important information it is always worth verifying them against the documentation or the platform’s support team.
